# Structural Drug Discovery Project - COBB 2035
Design and implement a small research project that aims to answer a question involving macromolecular and/or chemical structure.

Final deliverable will be a research poster, due 11:59pm Tuesday, December 8.
- All posters must be printed in time for a poster session from noon-2:30pm on Wednesday, December 9.
- Posters must be portrait orientation and no larger than 36" x 42". Background should be white.

Posters must succinctly yet comprehensively explain the research project, including providing necessary background and a clear description of the research question, methods, and results.
- OPTIONAL: provide a maximum of 5 pages of supplementary material (not including references)
- This supplemental material is not required, but as you are strongly encouraged to create a visually engaging poster that is not a wall of text or bogged down in extraneous detail, the supplement provides a means to provide information about your project that you would like to be considered during grading but that does not easily fit on a small poster (e.g., extended background, algorithms, code listings, proofs, additional supporting but not critical data analysis). The supplemental material is not expected to be a standalone research paper (i.e., you can assume the reader is already familiar with the poster).
